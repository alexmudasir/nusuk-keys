How it works

The same simple system as the community site: share first, then use other people's keys — and using keys fairly is what gets your own key seen.

1

Share your key first. Add it from the “Share your keys” tab — it joins the shared list straight away.

2

Use other people's keys by copying them, or tap Open in Nusuk so the key opens inside the official app, ready to claim.

3

Copied keys move to Used. Every key you copy or open is marked used and leaves the main list, so you always see fresh keys.

4

Use keys, don't just collect them. Copying many keys in a row without ever claiming them in Nusuk doesn't help anyone — claim the ones you copy.

5

The pinned key NG-PorCZWrx stays on top for every visitor, and the keys shared most recently keep rotating through the list for everyone.

Privacy Policy

How this site handles information, cookies and advertising.

What this site collects: Nusuk Keys does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or password. Keys you share are saved only in your own browser, on your own device — they are still there when you return. When you copy a key or open it in Nusuk, only that key's code is sent to the site's shared counter so the key is marked as claimed for everyone and the total claimed count stays correct. No name, email, address, or other personal information is collected or sent.

Cookies & advertising: This site shows ads served by third-party advertising networks. These networks and their partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can block or clear cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Third-party links: Key links open the official Nusuk service at services.nusuk.sa. This site is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

Contact: For any privacy question about this site, please raise it through the community where you found this page.
